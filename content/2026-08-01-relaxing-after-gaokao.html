高考后的放松

Relaxing After the Gaokao

高考 Gaokao放松 Relaxing游戏 Games

高考后，堂哥天天在家打游戏。中国学生学习太辛苦，考完需要放松。

After the Gaokao, his older cousin stays home playing games every day. Chinese students study so hard that they need a break after the exam.

Speakers: A - 儿子 son | B - 父亲 father
A: 为什么堂哥天天不出门，就在家里面打游戏？
wèi shén me táng gē tiān tiān bù chū mén, jiù zài jiā lǐ miàn dǎ yóu xì?
Why doesn't my older cousin ever go out? Why does he just play games at home every day?
B: 高考完就需要放松了。
gāo kǎo wán jiù xū yào fàng sōng le.
After the Gaokao, they need to relax.
B: 高中就天天学习！一周也不知道放假多久？
gāo zhōng jiù tiān tiān xué xí! yī zhōu yě bù zhī dào fàng jià duō jiǔ?
In high school, they study every day. Who knows how much time they even get off each week?
B: 这就是中国高考后现状。
zhè jiù shì zhōng guó gāo kǎo hòu xiàn zhuàng.
That's pretty much what happens after the Gaokao in China.
