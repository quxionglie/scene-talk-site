64.五月天长什么样子What Does Mayday Look Like?

2026-08-04·音乐 Music·乐队 Band·五月天 Mayday

孩子们看五月天照片，聊乐队里谁在唱歌。

The children look at a picture of Mayday and talk about who sings in the band.

Speakers: A - 弟弟 younger brother | B - 父亲 father | C - 哥哥 older brother
A: 五月天长什么样子？
wǔ yuè tiān zhǎng shén me yàng zi?
What does Mayday look like?
B: 我搜一下，看一下图片。
wǒ sōu yī xià, kàn yī xià tú piàn.
Let me look up a picture.
在网上搜一下"五月天"的图片
Search online for pictures of "Mayday."
B: 5个人组成的一个乐队。
5 gè rén zǔ chéng de yí gè yuè duì.
It's a band made up of five people.
搜到图片后，一起在看。
They are looking at the picture together after Dad finds it.
C: 这首歌是谁唱的呢？
zhè shǒu gē shì shuí chàng de ne?
Who sings this song?
歌曲《你不是真正的快乐》。
The song is "You Are Not Truly Happy."
B: 乐队中的一个人啊！
yuè duì zhōng de yí gè rén a!
One of the band members!
父亲并不认识主唱阿信，所以在图片中也不知道主唱是谁！
Dad does not know Ashin, the lead singer, so he cannot tell which one in the picture is singing.
B: 乐队有很多人的，有主唱，有吉他手……
yuè duì yǒu hěn duō rén de, yǒu zhǔ chàng, yǒu jí tā shǒu ……
There are several people in a band: a lead singer, guitarists...
