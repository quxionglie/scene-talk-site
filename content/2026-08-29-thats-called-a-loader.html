102.那叫铲车That's Called a Loader

2026-08-29·学校 School·施工 Construction

看楼下学校工地的工程车填坑，弟弟说小的是铲车，不是挖挖机。

While they watch construction vehicles fill a hole at the school site below, the younger brother says the smaller vehicle is a loader, not an excavator.

Speakers: A - 父亲 father | B - 哥哥 older brother | C - 弟弟 younger brother
A: 你还要看吗？
nǐ hái yào kàn ma?
Do you still want to watch?
B: 要。
yào.
Yeah.
A: 看了多久了？
kàn le duō jiǔ le?
How long have you been watching?
B: 看了十多分钟！
kàn le shí duō fēn zhōng!
Over ten minutes!
A: 你的挖挖机，你哥哥说看挖挖机能看一个下午。是不是？
nǐ de wā wā jī, nǐ gē gē shuō kàn wā wā jī néng kàn yí gè xià wǔ. shì bú shì?
There's your excavator. Your brother says he could watch excavators all afternoon. Right?
C: 可以看8个小时。
kě yǐ kàn 8 gè xiǎo shí.
I could watch for eight hours.
A: 你说小的是新的，是吗？小的挖挖机。
nǐ shuō xiǎo de shì xīn de, shì ma? xiǎo de wā wā jī.
You said the smaller one is new, right? The little excavator.
C: 那叫铲车！大的有点旧。
nà jiào chǎn chē! dà de yǒu diǎn jiù.
That's called a loader! The big one is a little old.
A: 喔，你那个是铲车。那边有大轮子的叫挖挖机。
ō, nǐ nà ge shì chǎn chē. nà biān yǒu dà lún zi de jiào wā wā jī.
Oh, the one you mean is a loader. The one over there with the big wheels is an excavator.
