97.跳绳信号线The Jump Rope Signal Line

2026-08-27·跳绳 Jump rope·信号 Signals

孩子在客厅用跳绳连接沙发和椅子，想象它可以传输信号。

The child connects the sofa and a chair with a jump rope, imagining it can carry signals.

Speakers: A - 孩子 child | B - 母亲 mother
A: 爸爸说：“客厅太凉快，我在这里睡了。”
bà bà shuō:“kè tīng tài liáng kuài, wǒ zài zhè lǐ shuì le.”
Dad said, “It’s nice and cool in the living room, so I’m going to sleep here.”
父亲在客厅的沙发上，正在午睡。
Dad is taking a nap on the living room sofa.
B: 随便他。
suí biàn tā.
Let him.
A: 所以我把线放到这边来了。
suǒ yǐ wǒ bǎ xiàn fàng dào zhè biān lái le.
That’s why I moved the line over here.
孩子把跳绳拉直，一头放在沙发上，另一头连到椅子上，用来传输信号。
The child stretches out a jump rope from the sofa to a chair and uses it to send signals.
A: 不然信号源都断了。
bù rán xìn hào yuán dōu duàn le.
Otherwise, all the signals would get cut off.
