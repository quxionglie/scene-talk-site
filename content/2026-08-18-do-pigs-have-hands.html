82.猪有没有手Do Pigs Have Hands?

2026-08-18·猪手 pig's trotter·双关 wordplay

孩子说猪只有脚没有手，父亲却拿“猪手”这道菜开玩笑。

The child says pigs only have feet, not hands. Dad jokes about a dish called "Zhu shou" ("pig's hands").

Speakers: A - 孩子 child | B - 父亲 father
A: 把手举起来！
bǎ shǒu jǔ qǐ lái!
Put your hands up!
B: 我四只手都举起来了。
wǒ sì zhǐ shǒu dōu jǔ qǐ lái le.
I've got all four of my hands up.
A: 这是脚！
zhè shì jiǎo!
Those are feet!
B: 那猪有没有手？
nà zhū yǒu méi yǒu shǒu?
Then do pigs have hands?
怎么区分手和脚？
How do you tell hands from feet?
A: 都是脚，没有手。
dōu shì jiǎo, méi yǒu shǒu.
They're all feet. No hands.
B: 不是有“猪手”吗？
bú shì yǒu “zhū shǒu” ma?
But what about "Zhu shou" ("pig's hands")?
“猪手”是一个菜名。
"Zhu shou" is the name of a Chinese dish.
