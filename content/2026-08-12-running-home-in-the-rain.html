73.冒雨跑回家Running Home in the Rain

2026-08-12·下雨 rain·打雷 thunder

昨晚打雷下雨了，一家人还不回家。

There was thunder and rain last night, but the family still didn't head home.

Speakers: A - 父亲 father | B - 孩子 child
A: 打雷的时候你们应该要回来了，还要我去接你们？
dǎ léi de shí hòu nǐ men yīng gāi yào huí lái le, hái yào wǒ qù jiē nǐ men?
You should've headed back when it started thundering. Did I still have to come get you?
B: 谁知道，别人牛牛都不知道。
shuí zhī dào, bié rén niú niú dōu bù zhī dào.
Who knew? Even Niuniu didn't know.
牛牛是邻居小孩的乳名。几个人当时在小区里一起玩。
Niuniu is the nickname of a neighbor's child. A few kids were playing together in the residential compound at the time.
A: 牛牛为什么冲回来吗？
niú niú wèi shén me chōng huí lái ma?
Why did Niuniu run back?
父亲问的是原因。为什么不是所有人一起跑回来？事实上淋湿了直接回家洗澡就行。
Dad is asking why Niuniu ran back instead of everyone running back together. Since they were so close, they could've just gone home and showered if they got wet.
B: 最大的雨的时候。
zuì dà de yǔ de shí hòu.
When the rain was at its heaviest.
但孩子没说原因，只说了时间。
The child doesn't explain why. He only says when it happened.
A: 然后你们等雨小了就回来了，是吗？
rán hòu nǐ men děng yǔ xiǎo le jiù huí lái le, shì ma?
So you waited until the rain let up, then came back?
B: 对啊，我们冲回来了。
duì a, wǒ men chōng huí lái le.
Yeah. We ran back.
躲雨地方离楼下的距离不到50米。
They were taking shelter less than 50 meters from their building entrance.
A: 冲回来？我还以为你妈抱你弟弟，然后跟你一起跑回来呢！
chōng huí lái? wǒ hái yǐ wéi nǐ mā bào nǐ dì di, rán hòu gēn nǐ yì qǐ pǎo huí lái ne!
Ran back? I thought your mom had your little brother in her arms and ran back with you!
B: 妈妈牵着弟弟，我是单独一个人跑回来的。
mā mā qiān zhe dì di, wǒ shì dān dú yí gè rén pǎo huí lái de.
Mom was holding my little brother's hand, and I ran back by myself.
