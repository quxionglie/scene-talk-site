96.角色有多高How Tall Are the Characters?

2026-08-26·游戏 Gaming·身高 Height

孩子问 CS2 游戏里的警察和土匪有多高，父亲觉得这个问题很偏。

The child asks how tall the Counter-Terrorists and Terrorists in CS2 are, and Dad thinks it's an unusual question.

Speakers: A - 孩子 child | B - 父亲 father
A: 这些警察土匪有多高？
zhè xiē jǐng chá tǔ fěi yǒu duō gāo?
How tall are these Counter-Terrorists and Terrorists?
B: 真人吗？
zhēn rén ma?
You mean the players?
是否是打游戏的玩家？
Do you mean the people playing the game?
A: 游戏里面的角色。
yóu xì lǐ miàn de jué sè.
The characters in the game.
B: 我不知道，我从来没想这些问题过。
wǒ bù zhī dào, wǒ cóng lái méi xiǎng zhè xiē wèn tí guò.
I don't know. I've never thought about that before.
B: 我觉得一定是一米八左右。只有你才想出这么偏的问题。
wǒ jué de yí dìng shì yī mǐ bā zuǒ yòu. zhǐ yǒu nǐ cái xiǎng chū zhè me piān de wèn tí.
I think they must be around 1.8 meters tall. Only you would come up with such a random question.
