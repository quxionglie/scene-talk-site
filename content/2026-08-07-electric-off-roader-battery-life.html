68.越大的车越耗电Bigger Cars Use More Battery

2026-08-07·玩具车 Toy car·电池 Battery·用电 Power use

电动越野车充一次电只能玩半个小时，车越大越耗电。

The electric off-road toy car only lasts half an hour on one charge. Bigger cars use more battery.

Speakers: A - 孩子 child | B - 父亲 father
A: 姐姐说她的车充一次电玩5个小时。
jiě jiě shuō tā de chē chōng yī cì diàn wán 5 gè xiǎo shí.
The older girl said her car lasts five hours on one charge.
B: 我感觉应该不可能啊，最多玩2小时差不多了。
wǒ gǎn jué yīng gāi bù kě néng a, zuì duō wán 2 xiǎo shí chà bù duō le.
I doubt it. Two hours tops, maybe.
B: 你这个充一次玩半个小时。
nǐ zhè ge chōng yī cì wán bàn gè xiǎo shí.
Yours only lasts half an hour on one charge.
B: 你这个车比她大一倍，越大的车越耗电。
nǐ zhè ge chē bǐ tā dà yī bèi, yuè dà de chē yuè hào diàn.
This car is twice as big as hers. Bigger cars use more battery.
A: 越大的车越耗油。
yuè dà de chē yuè hào yóu.
Bigger cars use more gas.
B: 越大的车越重嘛！
yuè dà de chē yuè zhòng ma!
Because bigger cars are heavier!
越重就越耗电或耗油。
The heavier it is, the more battery or gas it uses.
