57.手上拿一天Holding It in My Hand All Day

2026-08-02·玩具 Toys·遥控车 Remote-control car

孩子要带上刚买的遥控车，还坚持要一直拿在手上。

The child wants to bring the new remote-control car and insists on holding it in his hand all day.

Speakers: A - 儿子 son | B - 父亲 father
A: 等下带上遥控车！
děng xià dài shàng yáo kòng chē!
Bring the remote-control car later!
B: 放在行李箱里行不行？
fàng zài xíng lǐ xiāng lǐ xíng bù xíng?
Can we put it in the suitcase?
A: 我要在手上拿一天！
wǒ yào zài shǒu shàng ná yī tiān!
I want to hold it in my hand all day!
