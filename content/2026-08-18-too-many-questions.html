83.问题太多了Too Many Questions

2026-08-18·问题 question

孩子抱怨父亲不理他，父亲说他每天的问题太多，实在回答不过来。

The child asks why Dad is ignoring him. Dad says there are too many questions to answer.

Speakers: A - 孩子 child | B - 父亲 father
A: 怎么不理我啊？
zěn me bù lǐ wǒ a?
Why are you ignoring me?
B: 你的问题太多了。
nǐ de wèn tí tài duō le.
You ask too many questions.
B: 一天上百个问题，我都回答不过来。
yī tiān shàng bǎi gè wèn tí, wǒ dōu huí dá bù guò lái.
You ask me hundreds of questions a day. I can't answer them all.
