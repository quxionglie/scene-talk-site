131.茶叶是菜吗

Download PDF
2026-09-19·茶叶 Tea leaves·误解 Misunderstanding

孩子看到杯子里泡开的茶叶，以为那是菜叶。

The child saw the steeped tea leaves in the cup and thought they were vegetables.

Speakers: A - 父亲 father | B - 哥哥 older brother | C - 弟弟 younger brother
A: 不要碰我的杯子！
bú yào pèng wǒ de bēi zi!
C: 这怎么是菜啊！
zhè zěn me shì cài a!
看到杯子里的茶水。
He said this when he saw the tea in the cup.
A: 茶叶泡开了就是这样的。
chá yè pào kāi le jiù shì zhè yàng de.
B: 茶叶能吃吗？
chá yè néng chī ma?
A: 跟咖啡一样，12岁以后才能喝茶！
gēn kā fēi yī yàng,12 suì yǐ hòu cái néng hē chá!
B: 茶叶能吃吗？
chá yè néng chī ma?
孩子重复问了一句，父亲才理解他是在问茶叶能不能当菜吃。
The child repeated the question. Only then did the father realize he was asking if tea leaves could be eaten like vegetables.
C: 不能吃！
bù néng chī!
