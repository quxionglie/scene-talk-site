121.自己决定Decide for Yourself

Download PDF
2026-09-10·降温 Cooler weather·被子 Quilts

天气降温后，父亲借古诗告诉孩子，冷暖要自己感受，被子盖几床也由自己决定。

After the weather turns colder, a father quotes an old poem to tell his child that they can judge for themselves whether they feel warm or cold and decide how many summer quilts to use.

Speakers: A - 父亲 father | B - 孩子 child
A: 父母有两种。一种像你妈妈，她帮你做决定：你是盖一床，还是盖两床！
fù mǔ yǒu liǎng zhǒng. yī zhǒng xiàng nǐ mā mā, tā bāng nǐ zuò jué dìng: nǐ shì gài yī chuáng, hái shì gài liǎng chuáng!
There are two kinds of parents. One is like your mom—she decides for you whether to use one quilt or two!
A: 另一种像我这样，我把两床被子都拿出来，你自己决定。
lìng yī zhǒng xiàng wǒ zhè yàng, wǒ bǎ liǎng chuáng bèi zi dōu ná chū lái, nǐ zì jǐ jué dìng.
The other is like me—I get both quilts out and let you decide for yourself.
被子有薄有厚，薄的是夏天的空调被。
The quilts come in different thicknesses. The thin one is a lightweight summer quilt for air-conditioned rooms.
A: 你觉得哪种好？是你决定，还是你妈决定？
nǐ jué de nǎ zhǒng hǎo? shì nǐ jué dìng, hái shì nǐ mā jué dìng?
Which do you think is better—deciding for yourself or having your mom decide?
B: 盖个厚的！
gài gè hòu de!
I'll use a thick one!
A: 不是有一句古诗吗？
bú shì yǒu yī jù gǔ shī ma?
Isn't there a line from an old poem?
B: 《爱莲说》吗？
《 ài lián shuō 》 ma?
"On the Love of the Lotus"?
老师要求这一周背诵周敦颐的《爱莲说》。
The teacher asked the child to memorize Zhou Dunyi's "On the Love of the Lotus" this week.
A: 不是《爱莲说》，是“春江水暖鸭先知”。
bú shì 《 ài lián shuō 》, shì “chūn jiāng shuǐ nuǎn yā xiān zhī”.
No, not that one. It's "Ducks are the first to know when the river warms in spring."
A: 就是说：春天到了，鸭子都知道水是暖的还是热的。
jiù shì shuō: chūn tiān dào le, yā zi dōu zhī dào shuǐ shì nuǎn de hái shì rè de.
It means that when spring comes, the ducks can tell whether the water is warm or hot.
A: 冬天到了，冷还是热，你也是能感觉到的嘛。所以我觉得你自己决定好点。
dōng tiān dào le, lěng hái shì rè, nǐ yě shì néng gǎn jué dào de ma. suǒ yǐ wǒ jué de nǐ zì jǐ jué dìng hǎo diǎn.
When winter comes, you can tell for yourself whether you feel warm or cold. So I think it's better to let you decide.
