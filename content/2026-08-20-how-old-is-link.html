86.林克几岁了How Old Is Link?

2026-08-20·游戏 gaming·塞尔达传说 The Legend of Zelda·林克 Link

孩子问林克几岁，父亲说《塞尔达传说》系列时间跨度太大，无法确定。

The child asks how old Link is. Dad says The Legend of Zelda series spans too many different eras to know for sure.

Speakers: A - 孩子 child | B - 父亲 father
A: 林克几岁了？
lín kè jǐ suì le?
How old is Link?
A: 我一直都想问，就是有时候又忘、有时候又忘。我就等今天中午睡完起来才问。
wǒ yì zhí dōu xiǎng wèn, jiù shì yǒu shí hòu yòu wàng, yǒu shí hòu yòu wàng. wǒ jiù děng jīn tiān zhōng wǔ shuì wán qǐ lái cái wèn.
I've always wanted to ask, but I keep forgetting. So I waited until I woke up from my nap today to ask.
B: 我不知道！游戏都出来20年了，然后林克的跨度估计很大。
wǒ bù zhī dào! yóu xì dōu chū lái 20 nián le, rán hòu lín kè de kuà dù gū jì hěn dà.
I don't know! The games have been around for twenty years, so Link's age probably varies a lot.
《塞尔达传说》系列每款游戏的背景可能都不一样。
Each game in The Legend of Zelda series may have a different setting.
B: 你的公主还能从古代穿到现代，各个时代在穿。
nǐ de gōng zhǔ hái néng cóng gǔ dài chuān dào xiàn dài, gè gè shí dài zài chuān.
Even your princess can travel from ancient times to the present. She's been in every era.
A: 为什么啊？
wèi shén me a?
Why?
B: 主要是游戏出来很多款，游戏的跨度太大，死了也能复活，所以说我也不知道。
zhǔ yào shì yóu xì chū lái hěn duō kuǎn, yóu xì de kuà dù tài dà, sǐ le yě néng fù huó, suǒ yǐ shuō wǒ yě bù zhī dào.
It's mainly because there are so many games, and they span such a long time. Characters can even come back to life after they die, so I really don't know.
