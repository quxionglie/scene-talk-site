61.自己的生活自己负责Taking Responsibility for Your Own Life

2026-08-02·成长 Growing up·责任 Responsibility

孩子问堂哥天天打游戏为什么没人管，父亲说堂哥已经长大，成人要学会对自己负责。

The child asks why Second Uncle does not stop his older cousin from playing games every day. Dad says his cousin is grown up and can be responsible for himself.

Speakers: A - 儿子 son | B - 父亲 father
A: 为什么堂哥天天打游戏二伯也不管？
wèi shén me táng gē tiān tiān dǎ yóu xì èr bó yě bù guǎn?
Why doesn't Second Uncle stop my older cousin from playing games every day?
B: 都长大了，都高考结束了，考上大学了。
dōu zhǎng dà le, dōu gāo kǎo jié shù le, kǎo shàng dà xué le.
He's grown up. The Gaokao is over, and he got into college.
B: 自己都能对自己负责了。
zì jǐ dōu néng duì zì jǐ fù zé le.
He can be responsible for himself now.
B: 所以说你自己的生活你自己搞了，知道吗？
suǒ yǐ shuō nǐ zì jǐ de shēng huó nǐ zì jǐ gǎo le, zhī dào ma?
So you have to manage your own life, okay?
B: 以后你也这样。
yǐ hòu nǐ yě zhè yàng.
It'll be like that for you too later.
A: 真的吗？
zhēn de ma?
Really?
B: 父母后面给不了你多少建议了。
fù mǔ hòu miàn gěi bù liǎo nǐ duō shǎo jiàn yì le.
As you get older, your parents won't be able to give you that much advice.
