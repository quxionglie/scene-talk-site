62.高铁雨刷Wipers on Trains

2026-08-02·高铁 High-speed train·雨刷 Wipers

高铁外面下雨，孩子看到侧窗上水一直流，问为什么不装雨刷。

It is raining outside the high-speed train. The child sees water running down the side window and asks why there are no wipers.

Speakers: A - 儿子 son | B - 父亲 father
A: 为什么不装一个雨刷？
wèi shén me bù zhuāng yí gè yǔ shuā?
Why don't they put a wiper on it?
高铁窗户玻璃没有装雨刷，下雨时窗户外面水一直在流。
The high-speed train window has no wiper, and rainwater keeps running down the outside.
B: 这个是侧窗，侧窗不用装雨刷。
zhè ge shì cè chuāng, cè chuāng bù yòng zhuāng yǔ shuā.
This is a side window. Side windows don't need wipers.
B: 应该是前挡风镜才需要装。
yīng gāi shì qián dǎng fēng jìng cái xū yào zhuāng.
It's usually the front windshield that needs one.
A: 为什么这里都不装？
wèi shén me zhè lǐ dōu bù zhuāng?
Then why doesn't this part have one either?
用手指着自己的遥控车说。事实上不是所有车尾窗都有雨刷。
He is pointing at his own remote-control car. In fact, not all rear windows have wipers.
B: 没必要，装了还容易出故障。
méi bì yào, zhuāng le hái róng yì chū gù zhàng.
There's no need. If you add one, that's just one more thing that can break.
B: 就在前面影响你开车的地方装就行。
jiù zài qián miàn yǐng xiǎng nǐ kāi chē de dì fāng zhuāng jiù xíng.
You only need it in the front, where it affects the driver.
A: 为什么雨刷装后面它就会出故障？
wèi shén me yǔ shuā zhuāng hòu miàn tā jiù huì chū gù zhàng?
Why would it break just because it's in the back?
B: 什么东西都会老化、故障的嘛！
shén me dōng xī dōu huì lǎo huà, gù zhàng de ma!
Everything wears out and breaks eventually!
什么东西指的是所有物品。
Everything can age and break down.
