101.座位有点矮了The Seat Is a Little Too Low

2026-08-29·自行车 Bicycle·座位 Seat

孩子长高了，觉得自行车座位太矮，腿伸不开，在外面也骑不快，想请父亲帮忙调高座位。

The child has grown taller, and his bike seat now feels too low. He can't stretch his legs out or ride fast outside, so he asks his father to raise it.

Speakers: A - 孩子 child | B - 父亲 father
A: 爸爸，能帮我调一下车的座位不？
bà ba, néng bāng wǒ tiáo yí xià chē de zuò wèi bù?
Dad, can you help me adjust my bike seat?
B: 自行车吗？你想怎么调？
zì xíng chē ma? nǐ xiǎng zěn me tiáo?
Your bike? How do you want me to adjust it?
A: 现在座位有点矮了！
xiàn zài zuò wèi yǒu diǎn ǎi le!
The seat is a little too low now!
B: 我们现在去看看吧。
wǒ men xiàn zài qù kàn kàn ba.
Let's go take a look.
B: 你坐上来试一下。
nǐ zuò shàng lái shì yī xià.
Hop on and try it.
把车搬到客厅后。
After moving the bike into the living room.
A: 你看，腿已经到这里了。
nǐ kàn, tuǐ yǐ jīng dào zhè lǐ le.
Look, my knees are already up this high.
B: 好，我调高一下。
hǎo, wǒ tiáo gāo yí xià.
Okay, I'll raise it a little.
