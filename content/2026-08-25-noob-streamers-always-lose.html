94.菜播总是输

2026-08-25·游戏 gaming·直播 livestreaming

孩子想看打枪游戏，但今天无比赛，只能看菜鸟主播直播。

The child wants to watch a shooting game, but there are no matches today, so they have to settle for a noob stream.

Speakers: A - 孩子 child | B - 父亲 father
A: 我要看打枪！
wǒ yào kàn dǎ qiāng!
我要看CS2打枪游戏。
The child wants to watch CS2 gameplay.
B: 今天没比赛，你也要看吗？
jīn tiān méi bǐ sài, nǐ yě yào kàn ma?
EWC26比赛刚结束。
The EWC26 tournament has just ended.
A: 看。
kàn.
B: 只能看“菜播”了。
zhǐ néng kàn “cài bō” le.
“菜播”是直播平台的菜鸟主播。
"Cai bo" (noob streamer) is a joking term for an unskilled streamer on a livestreaming platform.
A: 怎么总是落后啊？
zěn me zǒng shì luò hòu a?
当前主播所在队伍大比分落后。
The streamer's team is way behind.
B: 菜播就是这样，总是输！
cài bō jiù shì zhè yàng, zǒng shì shū!
