116.谁能骑我的车Who Can Ride My Bike?

2026-09-05·自行车 Bicycles·承重量 Weight limits

兄弟俩早上骑自行车出门，父亲步行跟着，三人聊起谁能骑谁的车。

The brothers go out for a bike ride one morning while their father follows on foot, and they start talking about who can ride whose bike.

Speakers: A - 父亲 father | B - 哥哥 older brother | C - 弟弟 younger brother
B: 要不要骑我的车？
yào bù yào qí wǒ de chē?
20 英寸自行车。
The older brother is riding a 20-inch bike.
A: 你的车我骑不了。
nǐ de chē wǒ qí bù liǎo.
回家后查询才得知，这辆车的限重为 100 kg。父亲可以骑，
They only found out after checking at home that the bike had a weight limit of 100 kg, meaning the father could ride it after all.
A: 每辆车都有最大承重量！
měi liàng chē dōu yǒu zuì dà chéng zhòng liàng!
C: 那我的呢？
nà wǒ de ne?
16 英寸儿童自行车，最大承重（Maximum load）约为 30 kg。
A 16-inch kids' bike with a maximum load of about 30 kg.
A: 我也骑不了你的车，我一上去你的车就要塌了。
wǒ yě qí bù liǎo nǐ de chē, wǒ yī shàng qù nǐ de chē jiù yào tā le.
C: “重神”能骑我的车吗？
“zhòng shén” néng qí wǒ de chē ma?
对话发生时，HeavyGod 是 G2 战队的 CS2 职业选手。
At the time of the conversation, HeavyGod was a professional CS2 player for G2.
A: 我骑不了，他也骑不了。
wǒ qí bù liǎo, tā yě qí bù liǎo.
