146.哥哥飞走了

Download PDF
2026-10-03·兄弟 Brothers

弟弟说哥哥飞走了，哥哥却喊起了脖子痛。

The younger brother says his brother flew away, but the older brother calls out that his neck hurts.

Speakers: A - 父亲 father | B - 哥哥 older brother | C - 弟弟 younger brother
A: 你哥呢？
nǐ gē ne?
C: 哥哥飞走了。
gē gē fēi zǒu le.
孩子说哥哥的手就是“翅膀”。翅膀晃动过。
The child says his older brother's hands are his "wings". The wings moved back and forth.
B: 爸爸，我的脖子痛！
bà bà, wǒ de bó zi tòng!
在门口说话。
Speaking from the doorway.
A: 你发烧了？那你过来盖好被子。
nǐ fā shāo le? nà nǐ guò lái gài hǎo bèi zi.
B: 我又不严重！
wǒ yòu bù yán zhòng!
体温是正常的。
His temperature is normal.
