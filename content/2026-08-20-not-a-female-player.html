87.不是女选手Not a Female Player

2026-08-20·电竞 esports

观看 CS2 比赛时，孩子说一名长发选手很好看，父亲提醒他那是男选手。

While watching a CS2 match, the child says a long-haired player is good-looking. Dad tells him the player is a man.

Speakers: A - 孩子 child | B - 父亲 father
A: 刚才那个警察还蛮好看的。
gāng cái nà ge jǐng chá hái mán hǎo kàn de.
That police officer we just saw was pretty good-looking.
“好看”通常形容女生（也可以形容小孩）。
"Hao kan" (good-looking) is usually used to describe girls, but it can also describe children.
B: 哪一个？
nǎ yí gè?
Which one?
父亲还以为是现场装扮成警察的观众。
Dad thought the child meant a spectator dressed as a police officer at the venue.
A: 前面那个。
qián miàn nà ge.
The one up front.
A: 就是这个！
jiù shì zhè ge!
This one right here!
指的是比赛选手。
The child was pointing at a player in the match.
B: 这是男的，别以为留长头发就是女的。
zhè shì nán de, bié yǐ wéi liú cháng tóu fà jiù shì nǚ de.
That's a guy. Just because someone has long hair doesn't mean they're a girl.
