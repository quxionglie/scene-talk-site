123.冬眠不用吃饭No Need to Eat While Hibernating

Download PDF
2026-09-12·吃饭 Mealtime·冬眠 Hibernation

孩子叫父亲来吃饭，父亲开玩笑说自己在冬眠，一个冬天都不用吃饭。

The child tells Dad it's time to eat, but he jokes that he's hibernating and doesn't have to eat all winter.

Speakers: A - 父亲 father | B - 孩子 child
B: 爸爸，吃饭了！
bà bà, chī fàn le!
Dad, time to eat!
A: 我在冬眠。
wǒ zài dōng mián.
I'm hibernating.
B: 难道你不想吃饭了吗？
nán dào nǐ bù xiǎng chī fàn le ma?
Don't you want to eat?
A: 冬眠，一个冬天都不用吃饭。
dōng mián, yí gè dōng tiān dōu bù yòng chī fàn.
I'm hibernating, so I don't have to eat all winter.
B: 为什么，不吃饭不饿吗？
wèi shén me, bù chī fàn bù è ma?
Why? Don't you get hungry if you don't eat?
