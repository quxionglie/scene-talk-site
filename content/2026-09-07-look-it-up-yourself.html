118.自己查字典Look It Up Yourself

2026-09-07·近义词 Synonyms·字典 Dictionary·学习 Learning

父亲纠正自己昨天说错的近义词，并希望孩子以后尽量自己查字典。

A father corrects a synonym he got wrong the day before and encourages his child to look things up in a dictionary.

Speakers: A - 父亲 father | B - 孩子 child
A: “自然”的近义词，“一定”好像不太准！
“zì rán” de jìn yì cí,“yí dìng” hǎo xiàng bù tài zhǔn!
I don't think "yiding" (definitely) is quite the right synonym for "ziran" (naturally)!
短句是：“作为本地人，他自然知道这片森林中哪些地方蘑菇多。”
The example sentence is: "As a local, he naturally knows where to find lots of mushrooms in this forest."
A: 它是“当然”“理应”“固然”。写“一定”可能就不对了。
tā shì “dāng rán”“lǐ yīng”“gù rán”. xiě “yí dìng” kě néng jiù bú duì le.
Better synonyms are "dangran" (of course), "liying" (as one should), and "guran" (admittedly). Writing "yiding" might be wrong.
B: 你不说“一定”吗？
nǐ bù shuō “yí dìng” ma?
But didn't you say it was "yiding"?
A: 你要学会查字典了！
nǐ yào xué huì chá zì diǎn le!
You need to learn to use a dictionary!
B: 字典上有吗？
zì diǎn shàng yǒu ma?
Is that in the dictionary?
A: 这个要你自己理解。别人说的不一定对。
zhè ge yào nǐ zì jǐ lǐ jiě. bié rén shuō de bù yí dìng duì.
You need to figure that out for yourself. Other people aren't always right.
A: 老师为什么叫你查字典？你的《新华字典》还是有用的。
lǎo shī wèi shén me jiào nǐ chá zì diǎn? nǐ de 《 xīn huá zì diǎn 》 hái shì yǒu yòng de.
Why do you think your teacher tells you to use a dictionary? Your Xinhua Dictionary is useful after all.
B: 大的《新华字典》吗？
dà de 《 xīn huá zì diǎn 》 ma?
You mean the big Xinhua Dictionary?
A: 大的叫《现代汉语词典》。高中就是用这个。
dà de jiào 《 xiàn dài hàn yǔ cí diǎn 》. gāo zhōng jiù shì yòng zhè ge.
The big one is called The Contemporary Chinese Dictionary. That's the one you'll use in high school.
两本字典家里都有。
We have both dictionaries at home.
