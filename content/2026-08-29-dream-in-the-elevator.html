100.电梯里的梦|A Dream About an Elevator

📅2026-08-29·梦 Dream·电梯 Elevator

孩子梦见独自坐电梯被困，别人让他按铃铛。

The child dreams that he's trapped alone in an elevator and someone tells him to press the bell button.

Speakers: A - 孩子 child | B - 父亲 father
A: 我昨天做了一个梦。
wǒ zuó tiān zuò le yí gè mèng.
I had a dream yesterday.
B: 什么梦啊？能告诉我吗？
shén me mèng a? néng gào sù wǒ ma?
What did you dream about? Can you tell me?
A: 我坐电梯被困住了，别人让我按铃铛！
wǒ zuò diàn tī bèi kùn zhù le, bié rén ràng wǒ àn líng dāng!
I got stuck in an elevator. Someone told me to press the bell button!
B: 铃铛好像是紧急呼叫。
líng dāng hǎo xiàng shì jǐn jí hū jiào.
I think the bell button is for emergency calls.
电梯铃铛按钮是紧急报警按钮，被困或发生故障时按下求助。
The bell button in an elevator is the emergency alarm button. Press it for help if you get trapped or if the elevator malfunctions.
B: 你坐电梯一般不是跟爸爸妈妈坐的吗？
nǐ zuò diàn tī yì bān bú shì gēn bà bà mā mā zuò de ma?
Don't you usually take the elevator with Mom and Dad?
A: 我一个人坐的。
wǒ yí gè rén zuò de.
I was by myself.
梦里，爸爸妈妈没有和孩子一起坐电梯。
In the dream, Mom and Dad aren't riding the elevator with him.
