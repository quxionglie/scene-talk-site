109.放学要出门条You Need an Exit Pass to Leave School

2026-09-01·学校 School·出门条 Exit Pass

近几天放学离校要出门条，但孩子找不到老师开。最后只能跟同学去校医室开一张。

For the next few days, an exit pass is required to leave school at dismissal, but the child can't find a teacher to issue one. In the end, the child has to go to the school clinic with a classmate to get one.

Speakers: A - 父亲 father | B - 孩子 child | C - 保安 security guard
C: 出去要出门条，这几天都要。
chū qù yào chū mén tiáo, zhè jǐ tiān dōu yào.
You need an exit pass to leave. You'll need one for the next few days.
A: 老师在吗？要不要回班里拿一下？
lǎo shī zài ma? yào bù yào huí bān lǐ ná yī xià?
Is your teacher there? Should you go back to your classroom and get one?
B: 老师不在教室。
lǎo shī bù zài jiào shì.
My teacher isn't in the classroom.
A: 数学老师呢？
shù xué lǎo shī ne?
What about your math teacher?
B: 我不知道她的办公室在哪里。
wǒ bù zhī dào tā de bàn gōng shì zài nǎ lǐ.
I don't know where her office is.
A: 能让小孩今天先出来吗？老师都不在。
néng ràng xiǎo hái jīn tiān xiān chū lái ma? lǎo shī dōu bù zài.
Could you let my child out just for today? None of the teachers are around.
C: 要出门条才行。
yào chū mén tiáo cái xíng.
Not without an exit pass.
