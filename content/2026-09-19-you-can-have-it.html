130.送给你了You Can Have It

Download PDF
2026-09-19·杯子 Mug·礼物 Gift

父亲在厨房不小心打碎了平时喝水的马克杯，孩子把另一个杯子送给他。

The father accidentally broke his everyday mug in the kitchen, so the child gave him another one.

Speakers: A - 父亲 father | B - 孩子 child | C - 母亲 mother
C: 地上的用纸巾捡一下就行。
dì shàng de yòng zhǐ jīn jiǎn yī xià jiù xíng.
Just use a tissue to pick up the pieces on the floor.
父亲手滑没接好杯子。杯子没掉到地上，但碰到柜面就碎了，地面上有两块大碎片。
The mug slipped from the father's hand, and he couldn't catch it. It didn't land on the floor, but hit the countertop and broke, leaving two large pieces on the floor.
A: 现在想喝茶都不行了。
xiàn zài xiǎng hē chá dōu bù xíng le.
Now I can't even have a cup of tea.
C: 又要出钱买一个新杯子。
yòu yào chū qián mǎi yí gè xīn bēi zi.
Now we'll have to pay for a new mug.
C: 哦，厨房还有一个杯子，学校发的生日礼物！
ó, chú fáng hái yǒu yí gè bēi zi, xué xiào fā de shēng rì lǐ wù!
Oh, there's another mug in the kitchen. It was a birthday gift from school!
孩子的生日礼物，一直没用过。
The mug was a birthday gift for the child and had never been used.
A: 看一下。
kàn yī xià.
Let me see.
C: 在消毒柜下面。
zài xiāo dú guì xià miàn.
It's under the dish sterilizer.
A: 这个好看点。
zhè ge hǎo kàn diǎn.
This one's nicer.
一共有两个杯子，父亲挑了其中一个。
There were two mugs, and the father chose one of them.
A: 能送给我吗？不然我新买一个算了。
néng sòng gěi wǒ ma? bù rán wǒ xīn mǎi yí gè suàn le.
Can I have this one? If not, I'll just buy a new one.
父亲问孩子是否愿意把杯子送给他。
The father asked whether the child would let him have the mug.
B: 不用，送给你了。
bù yòng, sòng gěi nǐ le.
You don't need to. You can have it.
