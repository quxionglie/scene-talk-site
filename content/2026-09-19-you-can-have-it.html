2026-09-19·杯子 Mug·礼物 Gift

父亲在厨房不小心打碎了平时喝水的马克杯，孩子把另一个杯子送给他。

The father accidentally broke his everyday mug in the kitchen, so the child gave him another one.

Speakers: A - 父亲 father | B - 孩子 child | C - 母亲 mother
dì shàng de yòng zhǐ jīn jiǎn yī xià jiù xíng.
父亲手滑没接好杯子。杯子没掉到地上，但碰到柜面就碎了，地面上有两块大碎片。
The mug slipped from the father's hand, and he couldn't catch it. It didn't land on the floor, but hit the countertop and broke, leaving two large pieces on the floor.
A: 现在想喝茶都不行了。
xiàn zài xiǎng hē chá dōu bù xíng le.
yòu yào chū qián mǎi yí gè xīn bēi zi. ó, chú fáng hái yǒu yí gè bēi zi, xué xiào fā de shēng rì lǐ wù!
孩子的生日礼物，一直没用过。
The mug was a birthday gift for the child and had never been used.
kàn yī xià.
zài xiāo dú guì xià miàn.
zhè ge hǎo kàn diǎn. néng sòng gěi wǒ ma? bù rán wǒ xīn mǎi yí gè suàn le.
杯子有两个。父亲问孩子是否愿意把杯子送给他。
There were two mugs. The father asked the child if he could have one.
bú yòng, sòng gěi nǐ le.
