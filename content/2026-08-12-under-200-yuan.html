76.不会超过两百Under 200 Yuan

2026-08-12·行李箱 suitcase·价格 price

孩子问行李箱购买时间和价格，父亲说超过两百他不会买。

The child asks when Dad bought the suitcase and how much it cost. Dad says he wouldn't have bought it for more than 200 yuan.

Speakers: A - 孩子 child | B - 父亲 father
A: 爸爸，行李箱什么时候买的？
bà bà, xíng lǐ xiāng shén me shí hòu mǎi de?
Dad, when did you buy this suitcase?
B: 我买的时候你没出生呢！我都忘记什么时候买的了，很多年了。
wǒ mǎi de shí hòu nǐ méi chū shēng ne! wǒ dōu wàng jì shén me shí hòu mǎi de le, hěn duō nián le.
You weren't even born when I bought it! I don't remember when I got it. It was years ago.
A: 行李箱贵吗？
xíng lǐ xiāng guì ma?
Was the suitcase expensive?
B: 100多左右，不会超过200。
100 duō zuǒ yòu, bú huì chāo guò 200.
Somewhere over 100 yuan, but not over 200.
不超过200块人民币
No more than 200 yuan.
A: 为什么？
wèi shén me?
Why?
为什么是这个价格？父亲记不住购买时间，为什么还知道价格？
The child is asking why that price, and how Dad still remembers it when he does not remember when he bought it.
B: 超过200我不会买。
chāo guò 200 wǒ bú huì mǎi.
If it had been over 200, I wouldn't have bought it.
