65.酷霸王飞不起来Bowser Can't Make It Back

2026-08-04·游戏 Games·大乱斗 Smash Bros.·属性 Stats

孩子说酷霸王飞不回战场，父亲顺势解释游戏角色的力量和敏捷。

The child says Bowser cannot make it back to the stage, and Dad explains strength and agility in game characters.

Speakers: A - 儿子 son | B - 父亲 father
A: 爸爸，酷霸王飞不起来！
bà bà, kù bà wáng fēi bù qǐ lái!
Dad, Bowser can't make it back!
任天堂“明星大乱斗”里的酷霸王，被打飞后不容易回到战场。
This is Bowser in Nintendo's Super Smash Bros. After he gets knocked away, it is hard for him to get back to the stage.
B: 飞不起来就飞不起来嘛，谁叫他那么胖！
fēi bù qǐ lái jiù fēi bù qǐ lái ma, shuí jiào tā nà me pàng!
Then he can't make it back. That's what he gets for being so heavy!
A: 呵呵！
hē hē!
Hehe!
傻笑。
A silly laugh.
B: 你选容易回来的人就行。游戏里有一些属性，如力量、敏捷，像酷霸王力量就大！
nǐ xuǎn róng yì huí lái de rén jiù xíng. yóu xì lǐ yǒu yī xiē shǔ xìng, rú lì liàng, mǐn jié, xiàng kù bà wáng lì liàng jiù dà!
Just pick someone who can get back more easily. Games have stats like strength and agility. Bowser is really strong!
B: 除了力量、敏捷，你觉得还有其它吗？
chú le lì liàng, mǐn jié, nǐ jué de hái yǒu qí tā ma?
Besides strength and agility, what else do you think there is?
A: 还有反应。
hái yǒu fǎn yìng.
Reaction speed.
常见属性有力量、敏捷、体质、智力、精神。
Common stats include strength (STR), agility (AGI), constitution (CON), intelligence (INT), and spirit (SPI).
B: 反应也是敏捷的一种，敏捷越高反应越快。
fǎn yìng yě shì mǐn jié de yī zhǒng, mǐn jié yuè gāo fǎn yìng yuè kuài.
Reaction speed is part of agility too. The higher your agility, the faster you react.
B: 像 Joker 就是敏捷型的。
xiàng Joker jiù shì mǐn jié xíng de.
Joker is more of an agility type.
