58.空调开一天

Download PDF
2026-08-02·空调 Air conditioner·省电 Saving electricity

孩子问为什么空调要开一天，父亲说一直开比关了再开更省电。

The child asks why the air conditioner has to stay on all day. Dad says leaving it on uses less power than turning it off and on again.

Speakers: A - 儿子 son | B - 父亲 father
A: 为什么堂哥的空调要开一天？
wèi shén me táng gē de kōng tiáo yào kāi yī tiān?
B: 你堂哥都连续开一个月了。
nǐ táng gē dōu lián xù kāi yí gè yuè le.
B: 一直开比停了又开更省电。
yì zhí kāi bǐ tíng le yòu kāi gèng shěng diàn.
