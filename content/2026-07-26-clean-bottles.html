要干净的瓶子

I Want Clean Bottles

饮料 Drinks瓶子 Bottles

孩子天天想买饮料，其实是想要干净的瓶子。

The child keeps asking for drinks, but really just wants clean bottles.

Speakers: A - 父亲 father | B - 孩子 child
A: 你为什么天天说要买饮料呢？
nǐ wèi shén me tiān tiān shuō yào mǎi yǐn liào ne?
Why do you keep asking for drinks every day?
B: 因为我要瓶子。
yīn wèi wǒ yào píng zi.
Because I need the bottles.
A: 瓶子里的水怎么办？
píng zi lǐ de shuǐ zěn me bàn?
What about the water in them?
B: 倒掉呗……
dào diào bei ……
Just dump it out...
A: 楼下捡别人不要的，不行吗？
lóu xià jiǎn bié rén bú yào de, bù xíng ma?
Can't we just pick up the ones people threw away downstairs?
B: 我要干净的瓶子！
wǒ yào gān jìng de píng zi!
I want clean bottles!
