55.耳朵都带风了Wind in Our Ears

2026-07-31·电动车 E-bike·速度 Speed·农村 Countryside

在农村路上骑电动车，风吹得耳朵呼呼响。

They ride an e-bike on a country road, and the wind is whooshing in their ears.

Speakers: A - 父亲 father | B - 儿子 son
A: 我耳朵都听到呼呼声了！
wǒ ěr duǒ dōu tīng dào hū hū shēng le!
I can hear the wind whooshing in my ears!
B: 我也是。
wǒ yě shì.
Me too.
A: 我看了速度有40，也不知道对不对？
wǒ kàn le sù dù yǒu 40, yě bù zhī dào duì bú duì?
I checked. It said we were going 40, but I'm not sure if that's right.
40公里每小时。路上没人才可以这么骑。
Forty kilometers per hour. They only ride like this when there is no one on the road.
A: 我骑自行车下坡最多也就30多。
wǒ qí zì xíng chē xià pō zuì duō yě jiù 30 duō.
Even going downhill on my bike, I only get a little over 30 at most.
