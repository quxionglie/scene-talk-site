51.水气球Water Balloons

2026-07-29·玩具 Toys·水气球 Water balloons

孩子给爸爸看姐姐送的水气球，一个装了水，一个是空的。

The child shows Dad the water balloons the older girl gave him. One has water, and one is empty.

Speakers: A - 孩子 child | B - 父亲 father
A: 爸爸，来看水气球，一个有水的，一个空的！
bà bà, lái kàn shuǐ qì qiú, yí gè yǒu shuǐ de, yí gè kōng de!
Dad, come look at the water balloons. One has water, and one is empty!
B: 谁给你的？
shuí gěi nǐ de?
Who gave them to you?
孩子是空手出去的，晚上外面也不会有水气球卖。只能是别人给的了。
The child went out empty-handed, and no one would be selling water balloons outside at night, so someone must have given them to him.
A: 姐姐送的。
jiě jiě sòng de.
The older girl gave them to me.
B: 我看一下，水怎么打进去的？
wǒ kàn yī xià, shuǐ zěn me dǎ jìn qù de?
Let me see. How did the water get in?
A: 有一个水壶，能打水也能打气。
yǒu yí gè shuǐ hú, néng dǎ shuǐ yě néng dǎ qì.
There's a little pump. It can pump water and air.
