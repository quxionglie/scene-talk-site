114.人也会长满草吗Can People Become Overgrown with Weeds Too?

2026-09-04·荒地 Vacant lot·诗意 Poetry

早上在家，父亲和孩子从一块长满草的荒地聊到人身上的“土”和“草”。

One morning at home, a father and child talk about an overgrown vacant lot and wonder what “soil” and “weeds” might mean for a person.

Speakers: A - 父亲 father | B - 孩子 child
B: 为什么起来了？
wèi shén me qǐ lái le?
Why are you up already?
父亲比平时起得早。
The father is up earlier than usual.
A: 我想起你的工地了。
wǒ xiǎng qǐ nǐ de gōng dì le.
I was thinking about your construction site.
放学时，他们经过一块长满草的荒地。暑假几个月没见，草已经长到约50厘米高了。
On the way home from school, they passed a vacant lot overgrown with weeds. They hadn't seen it all summer, and the weeds were now about 50 centimeters tall.
B: 想这个有什么用？
xiǎng zhè ge yǒu shén me yòng?
What's the point of thinking about that?
A: 工地拆了房子，留下那些土在上面。几个月不打理就长草了。
gōng dì chāi le fáng zi, liú xià nà xiē tǔ zài shàng miàn. jǐ gè yuè bù dǎ lǐ jiù zhǎng cǎo le.
The buildings at the site were torn down, leaving bare soil behind. Leave it alone for a few months, and weeds start growing.
荒地上的房子在暑假前被拆除，建筑垃圾全拉走了，当时上面就只有土。
The buildings on the vacant lot were torn down before summer vacation. Once all the construction debris had been hauled away, only bare soil remained.
A: 然后诗人的角度就是：人几个月不打理，是不是也会长满了草？
rán hòu shī rén de jiǎo dù jiù shì: rén jǐ gè yuè bù dǎ lǐ, shì bú shì yě huì zhǎng mǎn le cǎo?
And here's the poetic angle: if a person were left untended for a few months, would they become overgrown with weeds too?
A: 但对于人来说，什么是土呢？然后什么是草呢？
dàn duì yú rén lái shuō, shén me shì tǔ ne? rán hòu shén me shì cǎo ne?
But for a person, what would the soil be? And what would the weeds be?
最关键的是，什么是打理？
The key question is: what does it mean to tend to a person?
B: 我都想去摘狗尾巴草了。
wǒ dōu xiǎng qù zhāi gǒu wěi ba cǎo le.
Now I want to go pick some foxtail grass.
