112.往年轻了叫Make Them Sound Younger

2026-09-02·称呼 Forms of address·年龄 Age

称呼别人时说得年轻些，对方通常会更高兴。

People are usually happier when you use a younger-sounding form of address.

Speakers: A - 父亲 father | B - 孩子 child
B: 爸爸，刚才那个人，我该叫他哥哥还是叔叔？
bà bà, gāng cái nà ge rén, wǒ gāi jiào tā gē gē hái shì shū shū?
Dad, should I call that man "gege" (older brother) or "shushu" (uncle)?
A: 哪个呀？
nǎ ge ya?
Which man?
B: 刚才发你传单那个。
gāng cái fā nǐ chuán dān nà ge.
The one who just gave you that flyer.
在路上推销课程的人。
He was promoting classes on the street.
A: 你叫他叔嘛。
nǐ jiào tā shū ma.
You can call him "shushu" (uncle).
A: 你叫哥也没问题。人家最喜欢你叫哥，说年轻点好听。
nǐ jiào gē yě méi wèn tí. rén jiā zuì xǐ huān nǐ jiào gē, shuō nián qīng diǎn hǎo tīng.
Calling him "gege" (older brother) is fine too. He'd love that. People like terms that make them sound younger.
B: 别说老的！
bié shuō lǎo de!
Don't make him sound old!
A: 你称呼别人时，说年轻点，人家就高兴。
nǐ chēng hū bié rén shí, shuō nián qīng diǎn, rén jiā jiù gāo xìng.
When you're deciding what to call someone, go with the younger term. They'll be happy.
A: “叔”是比较老的。
“shū” shì bǐ jiào lǎo de.
Calling someone "shushu" (uncle) makes them sound older.
正常来说，叔比哥年纪大。
Normally, a "shushu" (uncle) is older than a "gege" (older brother).
