106.锤菜Hammering Vegetables

2026-08-30·王国之泪 Tears of the Kingdom·武器 Weapons

父亲用锤子和菜刀解释不同武器的用途。

The father uses a hammer and a kitchen knife to explain how different weapons are used.

Speakers: A - 父亲 father | B - 孩子 child
B: 小岩石巨人为什么要用阿沅的刀才能打？
xiǎo yán shí jù rén wèi shén me yào yòng ā yuán de dāo cái néng dǎ?
Why do you have to use Yunobo's sword to beat a Stone Pebblit?
A: 有些刀是用来切的，有的刀是用来砸的。
yǒu xiē dāo shì yòng lái qiē de, yǒu de dāo shì yòng lái zá de.
Some weapons are for cutting, and others are for smashing.
A: 就像锤子和菜刀，你总不能用锤子来切菜吧！
jiù xiàng chuí zi hé cài dāo, nǐ zǒng bù néng yòng chuí zi lái qiē cài ba!
Think of a hammer and a kitchen knife. You can't use a hammer to chop vegetables!
B: 这就是“锤菜”了。
zhè jiù shì “chuí cài” le.
Then that would be “hammering vegetables.”
A: 以前我遇到小岩石巨人都是躲着走的，因为我的武器都打不了它。
yǐ qián wǒ yù dào xiǎo yán shí jù rén dōu shì duǒ zhe zǒu de, yīn wèi wǒ de wǔ qì dōu dǎ bù liǎo tā.
I used to avoid Stone Pebblits whenever I saw them because none of my weapons worked.
A: 后面才看到你把它举起来砸死。
hòu miàn cái kàn dào nǐ bǎ tā jǔ qǐ lái zá sǐ.
Then I saw you pick one up and smash it.
不用武器，靠近了按 A 键举起来再丢地上就可以了。
You don't need a weapon. Just walk up to it, press A to pick it up, and throw it on the ground.
