59.早上吃米粉Rice Noodles for Breakfast

2026-08-02·早餐 Breakfast·米粉 Rice noodles·点餐 Ordering food

早上在米粉店点餐，父亲点了几碗粉，又另外打包两份炒粉。

In the morning, the father orders luosifen, plain rice noodles, and rice noodles with meat, then adds two orders of fried rice noodles to go.

Speakers: A - 父亲 father | B - 堂哥 older cousin | C - 老板 boss
A: 一碗螺蛳粉，一碗素粉，你要什么粉？
yī wǎn luó sī fěn, yī wǎn sù fěn, nǐ yào shén me fěn?
One luosifen, one plain rice noodle soup. What kind do you want?
B: 肉粉。
ròu fěn.
Rice noodles with meat.
A: 老板，螺蛳粉、素粉、肉粉各一份。
lǎo bǎn, luó sī fěn, sù fěn, ròu fěn gè yī fèn.
Boss, one each: luosifen, plain rice noodles, and rice noodles with meat.
螺蛳粉8块、素粉4块、肉粉8块，一共20块。
The luosifen is 8 yuan, the plain rice noodles are 4 yuan, and the meat rice noodles are 8 yuan, 20 yuan total.
C: 好。
hǎo.
Okay.
A: 嗯，我先付一下。
en, wǒ xiān fù yī xià.
Okay, I'll pay now.
A: 老板，有炒粉吗？
lǎo bǎn, yǒu chǎo fěn ma?
Boss, do you have fried rice noodles?
菜单上有炒粉(12块)，但现在是早上，不一定有！
Fried rice noodles are on the menu for 12 yuan, but since it is morning, they might not have them yet.
C: 有。
yǒu.
Yes, we do.
A: 两份炒粉，另外打包。
liǎng fèn chǎo fěn, lìng wài dǎ bāo.
And two orders of fried rice noodles to go.
在高铁上吃。高铁上 50 块钱的盒饭太难吃。
They will eat them on the high-speed train. The 50-yuan boxed meals on the train taste awful.
