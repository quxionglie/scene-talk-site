113.X读作叉X Is Read as "Cha" (Cross)

2026-09-03·耳机 Headphones·字母 Letters

父亲和孩子在路上聊耳机，以及英文字母 X 在中文口语中常见读法。

A father and child talk about headphones and how the letter X is often called "cha" (cross) in spoken Chinese.

Speakers: A - 父亲 father | B - 孩子 child
A: 我戴上耳机，外面的汽车声音都听不到了！
wǒ dài shàng ěr jī, wài miàn de qì chē shēng yīn dōu tīng bú dào le!
I put my headphones on, and now I can't hear the traffic at all!
A: 听听歌，安安静静的多好。
tīng tīng gē, ān ān jìng jìng de duō hǎo.
It's so nice and quiet when I listen to music.
B: 你每次都喜欢戴耳机。
nǐ měi cì dōu xǐ huān dài ěr jī.
You always want to wear headphones.
A: 因为家里那个电梯太吵了。
yīn wèi jiā lǐ nà ge diàn tī tài chǎo le.
It's because the elevator in our building is too noisy.
A: 这款耳机都出到 XM6 了，我这个是 XM4。
zhè kuǎn ěr jī dōu chū dào XM6 le, wǒ zhè ge shì XM4.
They've already released the XM6. Mine's an XM4.
B: M4？跟枪的名字差不多。
M4? gēn qiāng de míng zì chà bu duō.
M4? That sounds kind of like the name of a gun.
指《反恐精英 2》（CS2）里的 M4。
This refers to the M4 in Counter-Strike 2 (CS2).
A: 索尼的 XM4，前面的 X，我们就读作“叉”（chā）。
suǒ ní de XM4, qián miàn de X, wǒ men jiù dú zuò “chā”（chā）.
It's Sony's XM4. We call the X at the beginning "cha" (cross) in Chinese.
因为对错的符号是“✓”（勾）和“✗”（叉），而 X 跟“✗”差不多。
That's because ✓, or "gou" (check mark), means correct, while ✗, or "cha" (cross), means incorrect. X looks a lot like ✗.
