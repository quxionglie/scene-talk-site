79.高中没专业No Majors in High School

2026-08-15·学校 school·专业 major

孩子问父亲高中学什么专业，父亲解释高中不分专业，大学才分。

The child asks what major Dad studied in high school. Dad explains that high school does not have majors; university does.

Speakers: A - 孩子 child | B - 父亲 father
A: 爸爸，大学你学计算机，高中学啥专业？
bà bà, dà xué nǐ xué jì suàn jī, gāo zhōng xué shá zhuān yè?
Dad, you studied computer science at university. What major did you study in high school?
B: 高中没专业，大学才分专业！
gāo zhōng méi zhuān yè, dà xué cái fēn zhuān yè!
There are no majors in high school. Majors start at university!
中国高中只分文科/理科。
Chinese high schools only divide students into arts and science tracks.
B: 高中学的是基础知识。
gāo zhōng xué de shì jī chǔ zhī shí.
In high school, you study the basics.
