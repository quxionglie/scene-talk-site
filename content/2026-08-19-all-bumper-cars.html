全部玩碰碰车

Bumper Cars Every Time

游乐场 amusement park碰碰车 bumper cars

父亲和孩子商量下次去游乐场时，如何用完门票的项目游玩次数。

The father and child talk about how to use up all the ride credits in their ticket package the next time they go to the amusement park.

Speakers: A - 父亲 father | B - 孩子 child
A: 下周可以去玩海盗船。
xià zhōu kě yǐ qù wán hǎi dào chuán.
We can ride the pirate ship next week.
B: 还要看天气。
hái yào kàn tiān qì.
We'll have to see what the weather's like.
A: 下次要把游乐项目全部玩完。
xià cì yào bǎ yóu lè xiàng mù quán bù wán wán.
Next time, we have to go on every ride.
B: 全部玩完？这么多项目，你玩得完吗？
quán bù wán wán? zhè me duō xiàng mù, nǐ wán dé wán ma?
Every ride? There are so many. Can you go on all of them?
A: 我们把次数用完。像海盗船，你才玩两次、三次吗？或者我们全部玩海盗船！
wǒ men bǎ cì shù yòng wán. xiàng hǎi dào chuán, nǐ cái wán liǎng cì, sān cì ma? huò zhě wǒ men quán bù wán hǎi dào chuán!
We need to use up all our ride credits. You could ride the pirate ship two or three times—or we could use all our credits on it!
门票套餐有次数限制。
The ticket package comes with a limited number of ride credits.
B: 我要全部玩碰碰车。
wǒ yào quán bù wán pèng pèng chē.
I want to use all mine on the bumper cars.
