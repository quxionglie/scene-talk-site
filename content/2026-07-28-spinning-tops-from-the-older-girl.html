49.姐姐带来的陀螺The Older Girl's Spinning Tops

2026-07-28·玩伴 Playmates·陀螺 Spinning tops

孩子说今天碰到了姐姐，还一起玩起了陀螺。

The child says he saw the older girl today, and they played with spinning tops together.

Speakers: A - 父亲 father | B - 孩子 child
B: 今天碰到姐姐了。
jīn tiān pèng dào jiě jiě le.
I saw the older girl today.
A: 你问了她这几天怎么没来吗？
nǐ wèn le tā zhè jǐ tiān zěn me méi lái ma?
Did you ask why she hasn't come down these last few days?
B: 她说这几天没出来。
tā shuō zhè jǐ tiān méi chū lái.
She said she just hadn't come out.
姐姐没说具体原因。
She didn't say why.
B: 我们玩起了陀螺！
wǒ men wán qǐ le tuó luó!
Then we played with spinning tops!
A: 有几个陀螺啊？
yǒu jǐ gè tuó luó a?
How many tops did you have?
如果几个人只玩一个陀螺，可能不够，所以会提出当前问题。
If several kids share one top, it may not be enough. That's why Dad asks.
B: 姐姐带过来了两个！
jiě jiě dài guò lái le liǎng gè!
She brought two!
