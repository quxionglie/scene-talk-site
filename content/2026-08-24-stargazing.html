看星星

Stargazing

星星 stars童年 childhood

从什么时候开始，看星星成为了一种奢望？

When did seeing the stars become a luxury?

Speakers: A - 孩子 child | B - 父亲 father
A: 昨晚我看到星星了。
zuó wǎn wǒ kàn dào xīng xīng le.
I saw some stars last night.
B: 农村以前夏天天气好，整个天空都是星星。
nóng cūn yǐ qián xià tiān tiān qì hǎo, zhěng gè tiān kōng dōu shì xīng xīng.
Back then, on clear summer nights in the countryside, the whole sky was full of stars.
A: 妈妈也是这么说。
mā mā yě shì zhè me shuō.
Mom said the same thing.
B: 上次带你回去，是因为下雨天阴天，所以没看到。
shàng cì dài nǐ huí qù, shì yīn wèi xià yǔ tiān yīn tiān, suǒ yǐ méi kàn dào.
Last time I took you back, it was rainy and cloudy, so we didn't get to see any stars.
A: 要不改天又回老家一次？
yào bù gǎi tiān yòu huí lǎo jiā yī cì?
How about we go back to our hometown sometime?
B: 可惜啊，你在这里不好看。城市里都不容易看得到。
kě xī a, nǐ zài zhè lǐ bù hǎo kàn. chéng shì lǐ dōu bù róng yì kàn dé dào.
It's a shame you can't really see them here. Stars are hard to see in the city.
“不好看”可以理解为：不容易看到星星，即使看到也只有几颗。
Here, “不好看” means that the stars are hard to see, and even when they are visible, you can only see a few.
B: 以前整个天空都是星星，还有流星！
yǐ qián zhěng gè tiān kōng dōu shì xīng xīng, hái yǒu liú xīng!
The whole sky used to be full of stars, and there were shooting stars too!
