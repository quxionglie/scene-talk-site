70.幼儿园长草了The Kindergarten Site Is Overgrown

2026-08-09·幼儿园 kindergarten·工地 construction site

楼下要修的幼儿园长草积水，可以养鱼了。

The kindergarten site downstairs is overgrown and full of rainwater. Dad jokes that they could raise fish there.

Speakers: A - 父亲 father | B - 儿子 son
A: 长草没有？
zhǎng cǎo méi yǒu?
Any grass growing yet?
B: 真的长草了。
zhēn de zhǎng cǎo le.
Yeah, grass really is growing there.
A: 可以养鱼了！
kě yǐ yǎng yú le!
We could raise fish there now!
B: 水是下雨积的，没法修学校了！学校都没法修了怎么办？
shuǐ shì xià yǔ jī de, méi fǎ xiū xué xiào le! xué xiào dōu méi fǎ xiū le zěn me bàn?
The water's from the rain. They can't build the school now! What if they really can't build it?
A: 旁边有个商场10年了都没建起来！
páng biān yǒu gè shāng chǎng 10 nián le dōu méi jiàn qǐ lái!
There's a mall next door that still isn't finished after ten years!
中国社会的现状：宣传与事实的巨大差距。
A comment on the big gap between what gets promised and what actually happens.
A: 都是骗子。
dōu shì piàn zi.
They're all scammers.
