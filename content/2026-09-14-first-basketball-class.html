孩子第一次上篮球兴趣课，因下雨只能在室内球场上课。

The child's first after-school basketball class had to be held on an indoor court because of the rain.

Speakers: A - 父亲 father | B - 孩子 child
xué dào yùn qiú méi yǒu?
（ diǎn tóu ）
A: 你们的同学很多吗？
nǐ men de tóng xué hěn duō ma?
sān shí gè.
sān shí gè quán bù bào mǎn le ma?
口误，“报满”应该是“到场”。两个词读音相近。
Dad misspoke. He said "fully booked" instead of "present." The two expressions sound similar in Chinese.
