104.为什么都写3Why Is 3 Written the Same Way?

2026-08-30·英语 English·阿拉伯数字 Arabic numerals

孩子看到中文和英文都写数字3，父亲解释阿拉伯数字全球通用，但读音不同。

The child notices that the number 3 is written the same way in Chinese and English. The child's father explains that Arabic numerals are used worldwide but pronounced differently.

Speakers: A - 孩子 child | B - 父亲 father
A: 为什么我们这里是3，英文里也是3？
wèi shén me wǒ men zhè lǐ shì 3, yīng wén lǐ yě shì 3?
如当前对话会分别显示中文、拼音和英文，但其中的数字都写成 3。
This dialogue is shown in Chinese, pinyin, and English, but the number is written as 3 in all three.
B: 阿拉伯数字是全球通用的。
ā lā bó shù zì shì quán qiú tōng yòng de.
B: 都是写3。但中文读“sān”（三），英文读 /θriː/（three）。
dōu shì xiě 3. dàn zhōng wén dú “sān”（ sān ）, yīng wén dú /θriː/（three）.
字写的都是3，但读音不一样。
It's written as 3 in both languages, but it's pronounced differently.
B: 你知道0、1、2叫作阿拉伯数字吧？
nǐ zhī dào 0,1,2 jiào zuò ā lā bó shù zì ba?
A: 知道。
zhī dào.
