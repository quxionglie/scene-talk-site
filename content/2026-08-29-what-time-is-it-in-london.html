103.伦敦现在几点What Time Is It in London?

2026-08-29·英国 United Kingdom·时差 Time difference

晚上在家看英超比赛时，孩子好奇英国为什么还是白天，父亲便查看了伦敦的当地时间。

While watching a Premier League match at home in the evening, the child wonders why it is still daytime in the UK, so his father checks the local time in London.

Speakers: A - 父亲 father | B - 孩子 child
B: 哪里的比赛？
nǎ lǐ de bǐ sài?
Where's this match being played?
A: 英国的，英超。
yīng guó de, yīng chāo.
It's in England. It's the Premier League.
B: 为什么都是英国的？
wèi shén me dōu shì yīng guó de?
Why are they always in England?
上周看了一场英超比赛。
They had watched a Premier League match the week before.
A: 英国的比赛好看啊！
yīng guó de bǐ sài hǎo kàn a!
English matches are fun to watch!
B: 这是白天吗？
zhè shì bái tiān ma?
Is it daytime there?
A: 是啊，我说过如果太阳在头顶就是当地中午12点。现在我们是晚上，欧洲是白天。
shì a, wǒ shuō guò rú guǒ tài yáng zài tóu dǐng jiù shì dāng dì zhōng wǔ 12 diǎn. xiàn zài wǒ men shì wǎn shàng, ōu zhōu shì bái tiān.
Yeah. I told you, when the sun is overhead, it's noon there. It's nighttime here now, but daytime in Europe.
B: 他们是多少点？
tā men shì duō shǎo diǎn?
What time is it there?
A: 我看一下。伦敦是14点。我们是晚上9点，他们是14点。
wǒ kàn yī xià. lún dūn shì 14 diǎn. wǒ men shì wǎn shàng 9 diǎn, tā men shì 14 diǎn.
Let me check. It's 2 p.m. in London. It's 9 p.m. here, and it's 2 p.m. there.
对话发生时，英国使用英国夏令时（BST），伦敦比本地时间晚7小时。
At the time of the conversation, the UK is on British Summer Time (BST), seven hours behind their local time.
