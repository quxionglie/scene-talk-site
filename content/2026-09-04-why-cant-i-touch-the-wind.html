115.风为什么摸不到Why Can't I Touch the Wind?

2026-09-04·风 Wind·科学 Science

孩子好奇为什么风能感受到，却摸不到也握不住。

The child wonders why the wind can be felt but can't be touched or held.

Speakers: A - 父亲 father | B - 孩子 child
B: 爸爸，风怎么摸不到？
bà bà, fēng zěn me mō bú dào?
Dad, why can't I touch the wind?
科学老师说风摸不到，孩子有点好奇。
The science teacher said that wind can't be touched, which made the child curious.
A: 是哪个风？是蜜蜂的“蜂”吗？
shì nǎ ge fēng? shì mì fēng de “fēng” ma?
Which "feng" do you mean? The "feng" in "mi feng" (bee)?
“风”和“蜂”同音，都读作 fēng。
“风” (wind) and “蜂” (bee) are homophones; both are pronounced "feng."
B: 窗户的风。
chuāng hù de fēng.
The "feng" (wind) that comes through the window.
A: 窗户的风不是能感受到吗？
chuāng hù de fēng bú shì néng gǎn shòu dào ma?
But you can feel the wind from the window, can't you?
父亲向自己的手吹风。
The father blows on his own hand.
B: 能感受到，但摸不到，也握不到手里。
néng gǎn shòu dào, dàn mō bú dào, yě wò bú dào shǒu lǐ.
I can feel it, but I can't touch it or hold it in my hand.
风也看不到！
You can't see the wind either!
