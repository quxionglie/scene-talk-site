108.不焦不躁Neither Anxious nor Impatient

2026-08-31·开学 New semester

开学报名时，学校给学生发香蕉，鼓励大家不焦不躁。

At registration for the new semester, the school hands out bananas to encourage the students to stay calm and patient.

Speakers: A - 孩子 child | B - 父亲 father
A: 我看你的书重不重？
wǒ kàn nǐ de shū zhòng bù zhòng?
Let me see how heavy your books are.
B: 背得我腿都软了。
bēi de wǒ tuǐ dōu ruǎn le.
They're so heavy my legs are going weak.
说明书包很重。
He means the schoolbag is very heavy.
B: 怎么发香蕉了？
zěn me fā xiāng jiāo le?
Why are they handing out bananas?
“焦”和“香蕉”的“蕉”同音，这里形成了谐音。
In Mandarin, 焦 is "jiao" (anxious), which sounds the same as 蕉 in "xiangjiao" (banana), creating a pun.
A: 喔，不焦不躁！
ō, bù jiāo bù zào!
Oh, "Bu jiao bu zao" (not anxious or impatient)!
父亲立刻看到了纸板上写的字。更常见的说法是“不骄不躁”。
The father immediately notices what is written on the cardboard sign. The more common expression is 不骄不躁, "bu jiao bu zao" (neither arrogant nor impatient).
B: 什么是不焦不躁？
shén me shì bù jiāo bù zào?
What does "bu jiao bu zao" mean?
A: 应该是不焦虑不急躁吧！
yīng gāi shì bù jiāo lǜ bù jí zào ba!
I guess it means not feeling anxious or impatient!
