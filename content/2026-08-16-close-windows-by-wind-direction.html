81.按风向关窗Close Windows Based on the Wind Direction

2026-08-16·下雨 rain·窗户 window·风向 wind direction

下雨时，要根据风力和风向决定关哪边的窗户。

When it rains, decide which windows to close based on how strong the wind is and which way it's blowing.

Speakers: A - 孩子 child | B - 父亲 father
A: 你这边还不是没关窗？
nǐ zhè biān hái bú shì méi guān chuāng?
But you didn't close your window either.
B: 我这边是南边，不用关。
wǒ zhè biān shì nán biān, bù yòng guān.
This side faces south, so I don't need to.
B: 你知道现在刮的是什么风吗？
nǐ zhī dào xiàn zài guā de shì shén me fēng ma?
Do you know which way the wind is blowing now?
A: 狂风！
kuáng fēng!
A strong wind!
B: 狂风？也对。但我说的是方向，不是大小！
kuáng fēng? yě duì. dàn wǒ shuō de shì fāng xiàng, bú shì dà xiǎo!
A strong wind? That's true. But I mean the direction, not how strong it is!
父亲其实想问的是南风还是北风？
Dad was actually asking whether it was a south wind or a north wind.
