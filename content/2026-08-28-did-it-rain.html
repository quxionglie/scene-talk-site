99.下雨了吗Did It Rain?

2026-08-28·下雨 Rain·洒水车 Street-cleaning truck

孩子用“下雨了”骗人，又解释马路是被洒水车淋湿的。

The child fools everyone by saying it's raining, then explains that a street-cleaning truck sprayed water on the road.

Speakers: A - 孩子 child | B - 母亲 mother | C - 父亲 father
A: 下雨了。
xià yǔ le.
It's raining.
B: 哪里有下雨吗？
nǎ lǐ yǒu xià yǔ ma?
Where? I don't see any rain.
母亲向窗外看了看。
Mom looks out the window.
A: 骗你的。
piàn nǐ de.
Just kidding.
C: 现在骗子真多。一个、两个、三个。
xiàn zài piàn zi zhēn duō. yí gè, liǎng gè, sān gè.
There sure are a lot of liars around here. One, two, three.
父亲一边说，一边按屋里的人数数。
Dad counts everyone in the room as he says this.
C: 下雨了，地板不是湿了吗？
xià yǔ le, dì bǎn bú shì shī le ma?
It did rain. The ground's wet, isn't it?
父亲还是不放心，向窗外看了看，发现马路是湿的。
Still unsure, Dad looks out the window and sees that the road is wet.
A: 洒水车洒的水，我刚才看到了。
sǎ shuǐ chē sǎ de shuǐ, wǒ gāng cái kàn dào le.
It's water from a street-cleaning truck. I just saw it.
C: 还真是喔！
hái zhēn shì ō!
Oh, you're right!
