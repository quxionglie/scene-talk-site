145.柚子哪来的？Where Did the Pomelo Come From?

Download PDF
2026-10-02·柚子 Pomelo

父亲问柚子哪来的，原来是楼下树上的。

The father asks where the pomelo came from. It came from the tree downstairs.

Speakers: A - 父亲 father | B - 孩子 child | C - 妈妈 mother
A: 我想问一个问题，这个是怎么来的？
wǒ xiǎng wèn yí gè wèn tí, zhè ge shì zěn me lái de?
Let me ask you something. Where did this come from?
B: 别人给的。
bié rén gěi de.
Someone gave it to us.
A: 还以为是你们买的呢！
hái yǐ wéi shì nǐ men mǎi de ne!
I thought you bought it!
C: 楼下奶奶给的。
lóu xià nǎi nǎi gěi de.
The older lady downstairs gave it to us.
A: 从楼下柚子树上戳下来的吗？
cóng lóu xià yòu zi shù shàng chuō xià lái de ma?
Was it knocked down from the pomelo tree downstairs?
小区里，几栋楼前各有一棵柚子树。
Several buildings in the complex each have a pomelo tree out front.
C: 是啊！
shì a!
Yeah!
