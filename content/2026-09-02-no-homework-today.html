111.今天是无作业日No Homework Today

2026-09-02·学校 School·作业 Homework

放学时，孩子说今天无作业日，并认为昨天的作业很多。

After school, the child says it's a homework-free day and that yesterday's homework felt like a lot.

Speakers: A - 父亲 father | B - 孩子 child
A: 你的书包呢？
nǐ de shū bāo ne?
Where's your backpack?
B: 今天是无作业日！
jīn tiān shì wú zuò yè rì!
Today's a homework-free day!
周三是无作业日，不带书包回家。
Wednesdays are homework-free, so the child doesn't take their backpack home.
A: 我还想问你今天作业多不多呢？
wǒ hái xiǎng wèn nǐ jīn tiān zuò yè duō bù duō ne?
I was going to ask if you had a lot of homework today.
A: 你觉得昨天作业多吗？
nǐ jué de zuó tiān zuò yè duō ma?
Did yesterday's homework feel like a lot?
B: 多！
duō!
A lot!
A: 多呀！你不是写了十多分钟就做完了吗？
duō ya! nǐ bú shì xiě le shí duō fēn zhōng jiù zuò wán le ma?
A lot? Didn't you finish it in just over ten minutes?
B: 不止十多分钟。
bù zhǐ shí duō fēn zhōng.
It took me longer than that.
