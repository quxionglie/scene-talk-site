120.跑不过老头子Can't Outrun an Old Man

Download PDF
2026-09-09·放学 After school·跑步 Running·秋雨 Autumn rain

秋雨中，父亲和孩子放学后跑回家，比平时早十分钟到楼下。

A father and his child run home from school in the autumn rain and reach their building ten minutes earlier than usual.

Speakers: A - 父亲 father | B - 孩子 child
B: 爸爸，等等我。
bà bà, děng děng wǒ.
Dad, wait for me.
A: 你一个年轻小伙子还跑不过我这个老头子。
nǐ yí gè nián qīng xiǎo huǒ zi hái pǎo bù guò wǒ zhè ge lǎo tóu zi.
You're a young guy, but you still can't outrun an old man like me.
B: 我拿着伞呢！
wǒ ná zhe sǎn ne!
But I'm holding the umbrella!
孩子撑着伞在跑，零星小雨，风有四级。
It's drizzling, and the child is running with an open umbrella in a force 4 wind.
A: 我还拿着你的笔袋和保温杯呢。
wǒ hái ná zhe nǐ de bǐ dài hé bǎo wēn bēi ne.
Well, I'm carrying your pencil case and thermos.
无作业日，不用拿书包。
It was a homework-free day, so there was no need to bring a backpack.
A: 你猜一下现在多少点了？平时我们都是三十五分左右到楼下的。
nǐ cāi yī xià xiàn zài duō shǎo diǎn le? píng shí wǒ men dōu shì sān shí wǔ fēn zuǒ yòu dào lóu xià de.
Guess what time it is. We usually get here at around 5:35.
两人一起到楼下时。
When the two reach their apartment building.
B: 17点25！
17 diǎn 25!
5:25!
孩子看到父亲手机上的时间后。
After the child sees the time on his father's phone.
