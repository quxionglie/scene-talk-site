110.以前随便出校门We Could Come and Go Freely

2026-09-01·学校 School·童年 Childhood

父亲回忆自己小时候学校没有保安，学生可以随便进出。

The father remembers that his school had no security guards when he was a child and students could come and go as they pleased.

Speakers: A - 父亲 father | B - 孩子 child
A: 我们以前有围墙，但门口都是开着的，你随便出！
wǒ men yǐ qián yǒu wéi qiáng, dàn mén kǒu dōu shì kāi zhe de, nǐ suí biàn chū!
Our school had walls, but the gates were always open. You could leave whenever you wanted!
B: 保安呢？
bǎo ān ne?
What about security guards?
A: 没有保安，哪有保安嘛！没人看到过保安，小学哪里有！
méi yǒu bǎo ān, nǎ yǒu bǎo ān ma! méi rén kàn dào guò bǎo ān, xiǎo xué nǎ lǐ yǒu!
There weren't any. Who had security guards back then? Nobody ever saw one at an elementary school!
A: 像大点的，叫作高小，也是随便出，因为厕所在外面。
xiàng dà diǎn de, jiào zuò gāo xiǎo, yě shì suí biàn chū, yīn wèi cè suǒ zài wài miàn.
When we were a little older, in what was called upper primary, we could still leave whenever we wanted because the toilets were outside the school grounds.
“高小”指高年级小学，即四、五年级；当时小学为五年制。
"Upper primary" referred to grades four and five, the upper grades in the five-year elementary school system used at the time.
B: 有小偷进来吗？
yǒu xiǎo tōu jìn lái ma?
Did thieves ever come in?
A: 那有什么小偷！
nà yǒu shén me xiǎo tōu!
What thieves? There weren't any!
A: 不像你们现在，一进去就围得严严实实的，哪里都不让出。
bù xiàng nǐ men xiàn zài, yī jìn qù jiù wéi dé yán yán shí shí de, nǎ li dōu bù ràng chū.
It's not like your school now. The whole place is tightly fenced in, and once you're inside, you aren't allowed to leave.
