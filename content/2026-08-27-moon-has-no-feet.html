98.月亮没有脚The Moon Has No Feet

2026-08-27·月亮 Moon·移动 Movement

孩子发现窗外的月亮在移动，父亲却解释不了移动原因。

The child notices the moon moving outside, but Dad can't explain what makes it move.

Speakers: A - 孩子 child | B - 父亲 father
A: 月亮怎么移动了！
yuè liàng zěn me yí dòng le!
How come the moon moved?
孩子在卧室玩耍，时不时看看窗外圆圆的月亮，发现它的位置在变化。
While playing in the bedroom, the child occasionally looks at the round moon outside and notices that its position has changed.
B: 太阳也会移动的。
tài yáng yě huì yí dòng de.
The sun moves too.
我们看到太阳东升西落，其实是因为地球自转。
We see the sun rise in the east and set in the west because Earth rotates.
A: 月亮没有脚，它怎么会动？
yuè liàng méi yǒu jiǎo, tā zěn me huì dòng?
The moon doesn't have feet. How can it move?
B: 我现在想，是不是太阳一动不动，我们地球绕着太阳转？
wǒ xiàn zài xiǎng, shì bú shì tài yáng yī dòng bù dòng, wǒ men dì qiú rào zhe tài yáng zhuǎn?
Now that I think about it, does the sun stay still while Earth goes around it?
父亲沉默片刻，没有正面回答。太阳真的是完全静止吗？
Dad pauses for a moment and doesn't answer the question directly. Is the sun really completely still?
B: 以后你可能会学到月亮为什么会动，我现在解释不清楚。
yǐ hòu nǐ kě néng huì xué dào yuè liàng wèi shén me huì dòng, wǒ xiàn zài jiě shì bù qīng chǔ.
Maybe you'll learn why the moon moves someday. I can't really explain it right now.
父亲知道和引力有关，可引力是什么，月亮又怎么运行？想想就头疼！
Dad knows it has something to do with gravity. But what is gravity? And how does the moon move? Just thinking about it gives him a headache!
