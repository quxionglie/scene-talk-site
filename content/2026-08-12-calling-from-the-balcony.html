阳台喊吃饭

Calling from the Balcony

吃饭 dinner阳台 balcony

父亲在阳台喊孩子回家吃饭，但孩子没听到。

Dad calls from the balcony for the child to come home for lunch, but the child doesn't hear him.

Speakers: A - 父亲 father | B - 孩子 child
A: 刚才我在阳台叫你们回家吃饭，有听到吗？
gāng cái wǒ zài yáng tái jiào nǐ men huí jiā chī fàn, yǒu tīng dào ma?
I was calling from the balcony just now for you to come home for lunch. Did you hear me?
孩子只是在楼下(下一层楼)邻居家玩，房子是同一个户型。
The child was just playing at a neighbor's home one floor down. The apartment has the same layout.
B: 没听到。
méi tīng dào.
No, I didn't.
A: 你们是在客厅还是房间里？
nǐ men shì zài kè tīng hái shì fáng jiān lǐ?
Were you in the living room or in a bedroom?
B: 有时候在阳台，有时候在客厅。
yǒu shí hòu zài yáng tái, yǒu shí hòu zài kè tīng.
Sometimes on the balcony, sometimes in the living room.
A: 按理能听到啊，可能你们太专注了。
àn lǐ néng tīng dào a, kě néng nǐ men tài zhuān zhù le.
You should've heard me. Maybe you were too focused.
