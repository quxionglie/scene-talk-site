大云和棉花糖

Big Cloud and Cotton Candy

云 Clouds棉花糖 Cotton candy

弟弟看到天上的大云，然后大家就聊起云和棉花糖。

The younger brother sees a huge cloud in the sky, and everyone starts talking about clouds and cotton candy.

Speakers: A - 弟弟 younger brother | B - 父亲 father | C - 哥哥 older brother
A: 爸爸，看大云！
bà bà, kàn dà yún!
Dad, look, a huge cloud!
“大云”是天上很大很大的云。
By "big cloud," he means a huge cloud in the sky.
B: 其它人很少会叫大云的，都是叫白云、乌云！
qí tā rén hěn shǎo huì jiào dà yún de, dōu shì jiào bái yún, wū yún!
People don't usually call it a "big cloud." They call them "white clouds" or "dark clouds."
中文里很少有人形容云为"大云"/"小云"
In Chinese, people rarely describe clouds as "big clouds" or "small clouds."
C: 云能吃吗？
yún néng chī ma?
Can you eat clouds?
B: 你不是吃过棉花糖吗？
nǐ bú shì chī guò mián huā táng ma?
You've had cotton candy before, haven't you?
父亲把棉花糖当作云。
Dad is joking that cotton candy is like a cloud.
C: 吃过！
chī guò!
I have!
A: 比我们家的白糖还要甜！
bǐ wǒ men jiā de bái táng hái yào tián!
It's even sweeter than the white sugar at home!
