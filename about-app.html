My Real-Life Mandarin

本网站发布的内容源于真实的日常对话，而不是虚构的教材情境。这些对话保留了日常生活里自然出现的小问题、新发现与误解。

这些对话不仅帮助中文爱好者——尤其是中文初学者——在真实场景中学习中文，也帮助英文初学者把身边的中文对话转化为自然的英文表达。语言学习是相互的：我们可以从中文学习英文，也可以通过英文理解中文。

中文文本是原作。英文翻译帮助读者理解原有场景的含义与语境。

The content on this website comes from real everyday conversations rather than invented textbook situations. These dialogues preserve the small questions, discoveries, and misunderstandings that naturally arise in everyday life.

These dialogues are intended not only for people who love Chinese—especially beginners learning through real-life situations—but also for beginning English learners who want to turn the Chinese conversations around them into natural English. Language learning works both ways: we can learn English through Chinese, and use English to better understand Chinese.

The Chinese text is the original. The English translations help readers understand the meaning and context of the original scenes.

SceneTalk Mandarin

SceneTalk Mandarin is designed for Mandarin beginners and travelers visiting China, helping you start speaking Chinese in real-life situations. It includes offline content such as scene-based dialogues, key expressions, cultural tips, background articles, and human-recorded Chinese audio, helping you learn common, natural Chinese expressions used in real communication.

The iOS app is available on the App Store in all regions outside mainland China.
